Primary Division

Architectural
Sheet Metal

We fabricate the full range of architectural sheet metal profiles for new construction and restoration — from parapet caps to full envelope systems. Shop drawings, custom profiles, and fast lead times on standard stock.

Work with your architect's specifications or send us a field measurement and we'll draft from scratch. Copper, galvanized steel, stainless, zinc, and lead-coated copper in stock.
