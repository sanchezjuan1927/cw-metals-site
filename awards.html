New York Landmarks Conservancy

Lucy G. Moses Preservation Award

The Lucy G. Moses Preservation Award is presented annually by the New York Landmarks Conservancy to recognize outstanding achievements in the preservation, rehabilitation, and restoration of landmark buildings and structures in New York City. CW Metals Inc. has received this award six times for metalwork fabrication and installation on landmark restoration projects.

Preservation League of New York State

Excellence in Historic Preservation

The Preservation League of New York State presents its Excellence in Historic Preservation Award to projects that demonstrate exceptional commitment to the principles and practice of historic preservation across the state. CW Metals has received this recognition twice for landmark sheet metal and ornamental work on significant New York City buildings.
